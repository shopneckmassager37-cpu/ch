About Chefaleh

Our Philosophy

We Cook With
Intention

At Chefaleh, we don't just focus on flavor — we focus on what goes into every ingredient. We cook exclusively with extra virgin olive oil and avocado oil — never seed oils.

We use unbleached, organic flours and avoid processed shortcuts. No soy-based fillers, no unnecessary additives, no compromises.

Real ingredients, properly sourced, cooked with intention. This isn't about trends — it's about quality, digestion, and how food should actually be prepared.

Built by a team with experience producing high-end culinary programs across the U.S. and Europe, our style is rooted in bold, clean flavors with an Israeli and Mediterranean influence.

Behind the Counter

Crafted by
Our Team

Every dish is prepared by our team of experienced chefs who understand that Shabbat is more than just a meal — it's a moment to gather, celebrate, and connect without the stress of cooking.

We bring restaurant-quality technique and farm-to-table principles to your home, ensuring that every element — from the proteins to the sides to the desserts — reflects our commitment to excellence.

Our kitchen operates with precision and passion, turning fresh ingredients into memorable meals that your family will talk about long after Shabbat ends.

Our Commitment

Consistency,
Week After Week

Every order goes through the same standards, whether it's your first Shabbat with us or your fiftieth — careful sourcing, small-batch cooking, and packaging that arrives ready for your table.

We keep our menu focused rather than sprawling, so every dish gets the attention it deserves before it leaves our kitchen.

That consistency is what our customers come back for, week after week.
