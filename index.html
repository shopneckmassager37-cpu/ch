Our Philosophy

We Cook With
Intention

At Chefaleh, we don't just focus on flavor — we focus on what goes into every ingredient. We cook exclusively with extra virgin olive oil and avocado oil — never seed oils.

Real ingredients, properly sourced, cooked with intention. This isn't about trends — it's about quality, digestion, and how food should actually be prepared.

Good to Know

Frequently Asked

Is Chefaleh kosher certified?

+

Yes. Chefaleh is ORB certified (Orthodox Rabbinical Board). Every dish is prepared in our certified kitchen under full supervision.

When do I need to place my Shabbat order?

+

Orders for this Friday close Thursday at 2:00 PM Miami time. Orders placed after the deadline are scheduled for the following Friday.

When is delivery?

+

Deliveries run every Friday between 3:00 and 5:00 PM, ahead of candle lighting.

Which areas do you deliver to in South Florida?

+

We deliver every Friday across South Florida: Miami Beach, Surfside, Bal Harbour, North Bay Village, Sunny Isles Beach, Aventura, North Miami Beach, Hollywood, Hallandale Beach, Fort Lauderdale, Pompano Beach and Boca Raton. Your delivery fee is worked out from your address at checkout.

What kind of food do you serve?

+

Our weekly menu is rooted in bold, clean Israeli and Mediterranean flavors. Everything is kosher, small batch and made to order.

Do you cater events as well as Shabbat?

+

Yes. Alongside our weekly Shabbat menu we cater private events, office gatherings and simchas across South Florida. Call (305) 985-0770 to discuss an event menu.
